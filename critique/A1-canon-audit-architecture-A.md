# Canon Compliance Audit — Architecture A, "Path-Primary Notation"

**VERDICT: REPAIRABLE** · **Canon fidelity: 4 / 5** · **BLOCKING findings: 3**

Commissioned separately because architecture A's audit failed to run inside the
Phase 1 workflow (its designer exceeded the structured-output retry cap after
writing the document). A is the architecture the Phase 1 recommendation selects
as structural base, so it was the one proposal whose compliance was unverified.

A does not commit the error that killed the previous attempt, and does not come
close. The builders are genuinely untouched, prohibited shortcut #14 is clean —
demonstrably so — and #8 and #19 are handled better than the handoff asks. The
three BLOCKING findings are one governance defect, one systematic omission across
the paradigm tables, and one logical contradiction under the document's answer to
its own central question. None require restructuring; all are fixable in Phase 1.

The document also argues for itself harder than its evidence supports. That
pattern — not any single violation — is the thing to watch as A becomes the base.

### #14 — verified affirmatively, not by absence

The highest-stakes clearance in this audit. The inventory and every attested form
were checked against Algonquian phonology. The profile is **actively
incompatible**, not merely different:

| Feature | Architecture A | Ojibwe / Potawatomi / Miami-Illinois |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Vowel length | absent | phonemic and central |
| Consonant clusters | none permitted | pervasive (`-shk-`, `-nd-`, `-mb-`, `-ng-`) |
| /r/ | present | absent |
| /tʃ/, /ʃ/ | absent | present and characteristic |
| Word structure | short CV(C) roots + slot affixes | polysynthetic verb complexes |

No attested form is traceable to Algonquian material. No Chicago-area vocabulary,
no toponymy, no morphological pattern. The in-world rationale — a 1908 committee
choosing syllables on acoustic-distance and channel-robustness grounds — makes the
phonology **diegetically human by construction**, removing the motive to reach for
a real source. A also withholds the find-site register specifically so the design
cannot be read as equating the burial mound with alien authorship.

### #2 / #18 — the same defect measured two ways

A names the risk itself: *"A grammar that encodes network topology this cleanly
makes the Box look more decipherable than canon wants… This is the failure mode
this architecture will actually die of if it dies."* Correct diagnosis,
insufficient countermeasures.

The residue A relies on is **entirely lexical**. The **grammar is presented as
~100% recovered**: of roughly 33 formatives, exactly three carry any live
uncertainty. A corpus of 4,100 tokens with no bilingual yielding a *complete and
uncontested* functional morphology is not the Etruscan situation A claims — it is
better than Etruscan, which is what makes it read as an answer key.

At the **example** layer A is fully compliant and genuinely good (G-grades,
competing readings, a G4 minority view). At the **paradigm** layer it fails
completely: not one row in the edge-class, incidence, grade, polarity, warrant, or
coordinate tables carries a confidence grade or competing reading. **The G0–G5
system A invented is never applied to the grammar it grades everything else with.**

This is direct non-compliance with handoff §5.3: *"every foundational claim should
carry a confidence level and at least one plausible competing interpretation."*

## Part 2 — Authority discipline

### BLOCKING-1 — the invented authority level "D-resolved"

Two rows of the assumption ledger are stamped `D-resolved`. **That is not a level
in the authority model.** D means *"deliberately undecided; offer options, do not
silently decide."* No mechanism converts a D to resolved except the user.

Worse, both rows land on explicit handoff §9.2 open questions — corpus size, and
whether the substrate is written/acoustic/EM/spatial/multimodal — and are filed as
**production-only with "no cosmological exposure"**, when the four-channel claim
asserts the network produces recordable phenomena in three distinct physical
registers. A cosmological claim, in the non-cosmological table, under a counterfeit
label.

BLOCKING on governance grounds: the authority model is the project's single
control mechanism, and a document that unilaterally extends it has disabled the
control. The *substance* is defensible; the *label* is illegitimate.

### BLOCKING-3 — the conditioned-erosion contradiction

Two load-bearing statements that cannot both be true:

- §7: *"Head halves regularly lost before **vowel-initial** roots."*
- §6.1: *"The phonetic form of this language is HEL's, not the corpus's… Nothing
  about the sound of `tanuli` is evidence about anything."*

"Vowel-initial" is a property of the 1908 Transcription Standard, which §6.1 says
carries no information about the source. Either the transliteration's vowels do
reflect source phonology — contradicting §6.1 and establishing what handoff §5.3
forbids, whether the substrate was biologically spoken — or the conditioning
environment is an artifact of HEL's own transcription, in which case it is **not
evidence of erosion at all** and the strongest leg of A's language-hood argument
collapses.

**Recommended repair takes the second branch and makes the ambiguity the content:**
restate the environment as explicitly graphotactic, and wire it to material A
already has (the admission that ~30 units are probably mis-segmented since 1908).
If the segmentation is wrong, the conditioning may be an illusion produced by HEL's
own transcription — meaning **the best evidence that this is a language may be an
artifact of the tool used to write it down.**

### Further unlabeled cosmology

- **Shadow segments** — the corpus recording traversals that did not occur, implying
  deliberation or predictive capacity. Absent from the cosmology table. The
  statistically likeliest deflation (misparsed damage, given that ~41% of sequences
  terminate in a broken half) is never reached.
- **AUTH performativity** — Field Protocol restricting AUTH forms spoken aloud near
  an active locus puts human utterance as a possible network input on the table,
  which is handoff §9.2's open question. Excellently hedged in place, but absent
  from the ledger.

## Part 3 — Silently resolved Authority D questions

| Question | Verdict |
| --- | --- |
| The builders | **Not resolved.** Best-defended area. |
| The Box's purpose | **Not resolved** — not approached at all. |
| Whether the substrate was biologically spoken | **Not resolved**, but BLOCKING-3 leaks toward "yes". |
| Whether the corpus is one system | **Effectively resolved by demonstration.** |
| The entities' nature | **Not resolved.** Explicitly declined. |

**The Palimpsest school is asserted and then refuted by the document's own worked
examples.** Two examples from different channels parse cleanly against the same
paradigm, morpheme for morpheme — demonstrating that the channels share a grammar.
The highest-value available addition: **a sequence the paradigm fails to parse**,
showing the Archive's forced reading and where it breaks. That would be the only
place in the document where the reader watches HEL's method fail in real time.

**The "grammar predicts this" overclaim.** A calls it *"the strongest single
argument on this document"*: that its coordinate makes "return to 2024 may emerge
in Los Angeles" a theorem. The inference does not hold. The coordinate entails that
**the record is silent about locus**; it cannot entail that **the traveler emerges
elsewhere**. A grammar cannot predict a physical fact, only mirror one. Left
standing, it licenses reading the substrate as a true model of the network's
physics — the shortest route to prohibited shortcut #2.

*Repair as structural congruence:* the coordinate is congruent with the canon fact,
HEL has noticed, and the two schools read the congruence in opposite directions.
This preserves the elegance and **converts the document's strongest argument into
its strongest unresolved question.**

## Part 4 — Explanatory resistance

**The Box retains it. The network largely does not.** A takes no corpus from the
casing and asserts nothing about it. But between seven edge classes, four incidence
markers, four-way polarity and the three-slot coordinate, a complete segment **is**
an answer key for the edge it describes. Every counterweight A cites is lexical and
none impedes route reading.

*Strongest single repair:* **put a blank in the load-bearing position.** Let the
PHASE "measured" values encode a magnitude HEL cannot recover — the contrast is
formally secure, the unit and quantity are not. HEL then knows a displacement was
measured, and by what sign, and never by how much. Keeps every canon derivation
intact, makes every recovered route under-determined in the dimension travelers
care most about, and explains why a century of operational correlation produced
G2 rather than certainty.

## Part 5 — Topology stress-test

**Q: Could a speaker express a reversible journey as easily as a legal one?**
A false assertion is formable — but that is not what #5 prohibits, and handoff §6.1
affirmatively *requires* hypothetical reverse motion to be expressible. What matters
is whether an illegal claim is marked as cheaply as a legal one. It is not:
orientation is obligatory, a half-edge is visibly broken, dedicated machinery
exists for barred and non-existent edges, and a hypothetical differs from an
attestation in three independent places. **PASS.**

Two qualifications: the reversal edit is mechanically cheap (one CV swap), which is
the unacknowledged price of read-order invariance; and **the PHASE anchor is never
defined anywhere in the document**. Anchor it to the tail-bearing origin node and a
reversed chain's coordinate becomes structurally invalid rather than merely false —
an illegal reversal becomes *ungrammatical*. Fix the anchor and #5 is not just clean
but structurally guaranteed.

**Q: Could a bare year identify a destination?**
**No — impossible rather than discouraged.** There is no absolute-time slot; all
PHASE values are relative, so a year is not expressible in the first place. And a
temporal specification with unknown destination is **required to announce that it
does not** carry one, via the obligatory gap marker. **PASS, emphatically.** The
obligatory-gap marker is the single most valuable invention in Architecture A.

## Part 6 — Conceptual alienness vs. orthographic costume

**Split verdict: conceptually alien, phonaesthetically generic.**

Clean on the forbidden costume list, verified mechanically: zero apostrophes in any
language material, no clusters, no honorifics.

The alien quality is genuinely conceptual and load-bearing — the **bipartite edge**
(one morpheme split across two words, orientation carried by which half lands
where; no human language does this), **obligatory overt ignorance-marking**, the
absence of verb/subject/possessive, and a coordinate that does not map to
where/when.

**But the surface forms are not alien.** Six of seven sample forms are entirely
open CV syllables; only one uses a coda, despite codas being licensed. The
inventory *permits* a distinctive silhouette and the lexicon declines to use it.
Read aloud, it is pleasant, pronounceable and unremarkable — it sounds like a
well-behaved constructed vocabulary, not something recovered under uncertainty.

*Repairs:* publish the 1908 committee's sanctioned syllable list and build every
unit from it; impose a minimum coda rate; add a distinctness rule with in-world
teeth (no two core units share their first CV) — which also explains why the
readback checksum ritual exists.

## Part 7 — Is anything a human language wearing a hat?

**The system, no. The transliteration, yes — and not the language you would fear.**

The inventory is close to a clone of Japanese, and the sample lexicon lands on it
audibly: **`otera` is Japanese お寺 "temple"**, and **`hemasu` ends in `-masu`**, the
most recognizable Japanese verb suffix on earth.

So the document's claim — *"No real-world language supplied phonological material,
texture, or 'flavor'… chosen by acoustic-distance and channel-robustness criteria
and by nothing else"* — **is falsified by its own output.** Made in good faith; the
execution drifted and nobody checked the result against the assertion.

**SERIOUS as a truthfulness defect in a compliance claim, MINOR as a design
defect.** Nothing here touches #14 — Japanese is not Indigenous North American
material — but a document whose credibility rests on self-audit cannot make a
factual claim about itself that its own examples contradict.

*Repair:* soften the global claim to what is defensible, then add the narrow claim
that is true and verified: the inventory was screened against Algonquian,
Anishinaabe, Potawatomi and Miami-Illinois phonology and is incompatible with all
four. A vulnerable global claim becomes a narrow, verified, far more valuable one.

## Part 8 — Is this still a language?

### It is notation. Specifically, a provenance-stamped graph-serialization format.

Every clause is `node-state + edge-half + incidence + grade + polarity + coordinate
+ warrant`. No predication, no reference to individuals, no modification, no
coordination, no subordination beyond warrant scope. Nothing sayable that is not a
graph fact plus a provenance stamp. A concedes the decisive point itself:

> **"Not one segment in the corpus does anything a poem does."**
> *"There is no attested way to express an interior state, a wish, a memory, an
> address to another party, a comparison, a metaphor, or a judgment of value… If a
> writer sits down to write Namárië in this system, there is nothing to write it with."*

The four properties A offers as counter-evidence were tested. Three do not survive:
obligatory non-informative categories **fail to discriminate** (a CSV with a
mandatory NULL token has the property); structural ambiguity is **supported by one
instance**, and that one arises from transmission damage rather than design;
conditioned erosion is **internally contradictory** (BLOCKING-3); and
non-compositionality — the property that would most decisively establish
language-hood — is **asserted with zero supporting instances anywhere in 957 lines.**

### Is notation defensible as the Box's control syntax? **Yes — emphatically.**

**A grammar with no obligatory subject position cannot name a builder.** That is not
a policy, it is a structural impossibility, and it is worth a great deal to a
project that already lost one attempt to exactly that failure. Four ideas here —
the Three-School Test, the obligatory ignorance-marker, the mandatory warrant slot,
and truncated citation as native redaction — are the strongest canon-protective
machinery the project has produced and should survive whatever else changes.

### Does it forfeit the resonance needed to rival Tolkien? **On its own, yes.**

The frame does carry much of Tolkien's weight. But the frame works **because the
object inside it can be loved.** Namárië is a text *in* Quenya; the grief is
available in the artifact, not only in the narration wrapped around it.

The pathos A offers — reading under uncertainty, a warrant nobody can pronounce
said aloud by every crew that goes out, eighty years of argument over a plaque — is
genuinely moving and exactly right for HEL. **But it is the pathos of a philology,
not of a language.** Those are different products, and the project should not be
allowed to believe it is buying the first when it is buying the second.

**A is the right skeleton and the wrong whole.**

### The sentence that must not stand

> *"Whether anything goes in them is a decision for a later phase and possibly for
> a later architecture."*

Left standing, **that sentence guarantees the answer is no.** It also fails the
brief on its own terms: a gap must correspond to something actually designed and
deliberately withheld, never to something we failed to work out.

**Required repairs, with a named owner, before Phase 2:**

1. **Strike the sentence.** The non-topological chambers become a Phase 2
   deliverable of equal rank with the edge classes.
2. **Redefine Stratum III — the largest unexploited asset in the document.**
   Currently defined vacuously and constituting **17% of the corpus doing no work
   whatsoever.** Redefine it as material whose morphology **resolves to no edge,
   incidence, grade, polarity or coordinate function at all**, and which the Archive
   therefore cannot parse. That yields, by construction, *the part that isn't
   topology*: canon-perfect (untranslatable, resistant, fragmentary), free today,
   and **the only place in A's design where a future writer can put something that
   is not a graph fact.** Right now that place does not exist — which is the actual
   reason A cannot carry a lament. Not the absence of poetry, but the absence of
   anywhere to put it.
3. **Pair the architecture**, taking A's own closing line at face value: *"If the
   product needs a language a fan can fall in love with and write poetry in,
   Path-Primary is insufficient on its own and should be paired with something else
   — or not chosen."*
