# Working Brief — HEL Fictional Language Project

**Supersedes:** the initial draft of this file, which was written before the canon
handoff was available and invented an alien civilization. That premise is void.

**Authority:** `docs/00-CANON-HANDOFF.md` governs. Where this brief and the canon
handoff disagree, the handoff wins. This file exists only to translate the handoff
into working instructions for contributors.

---

## 0. Read this first

The canon handoff uses a four-level authority model. Internalize it:

| Level | Meaning | What you may do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **A** | Established canon | Build on it; never contradict it |
| **B** | Working canon | Use provisionally; flag the dependency |
| **C** | Recommendation | Develop it, but label it for approval |
| **D** | Open | Offer options; **do not silently decide** |

**The central discipline: plausibility is not canon.** If you find yourself
reasoning "it would make sense that…", stop. That is exactly the move that
produces canon violations. Label it C and move on.

## 1. What is established (Authority A — never contradict)

- The **Horizon Expedition League** is an ancient, secretive organization centered
  in Chicago, embedded in ordinary institutions. Stewardship under radical
  uncertainty. Not a conquest org, not a startup, not omniscient.
- Its origin ties to a **Native American burial mound or hotspot** near the mouth
  of the Chicago River. Causality unknown.
- The **Black Box** is a prehuman extraterrestrial artifact, permanently encased,
  visually unspecified, fundamentally not understood, that enables timespace travel.
  "Black Box" is a *human operational label*.
- **Streams** are fixed/constrained, **one-directional**, interlocking loops across
  space and time. Departure and arrival are asymmetric. A→B may require A→C→B.
  A date does not determine a place: "return to 2024" may emerge in Los Angeles.
- **HEL's mission:** explore, document, protect, contain, prevent weaponization
  and destabilization.
- **HEL culture:** science + archaeology + intelligence tradecraft + inherited myth
  + superstition + protective ritual + secrecy + documentation + stewardship.
  Do not flatten this into either "hard science agency" or "occult order."
- **PNP lore is fully retconned out.** No Culture Codex. No PNP proper nouns.

## 2. What is open (Authority D — do not fill silently)

The Black Box builders. Their appearance, era, motive, biology, and whether they
spoke at all. Why the network exists. Whether the substrate was ever spoken
biologically. Whether the recovered corpus is even one system. The entities'
nature, origin, and communicative capacity.

**There is no established alien civilization and no native speaker community.**
Any design that requires one is out of scope.

## 3. The nineteen prohibited shortcuts

Reproduced from handoff §8 because these are the most likely failure modes.
Violating any of these is a blocking defect, not a style note.

1. Name or describe the Black Box builders as established fact
2. Make the Black Box fully decipherable
3. Turn "Black Box" into a confirmed alien self-designation
4. Create unrestricted portals or reversible time travel
5. Let grammar erase the fixed, one-way network topology
6. Imply that a year alone identifies a destination
7. Convert HEL into a public galactic government or conquering military
8. Make HEL omniscient or uniformly correct
9. Import PNP lore or the Culture Codex
10. Treat Standard Egg as a canonical alien term or final form
11. Make the NFC pin an egg, soul container, summoning object, or genetic key
12. Assume entities are property
13. Establish an entity language before settling what the entities are
14. Borrow a real Indigenous language to imply it is extraterrestrial
15. Equate the Chicago burial-mound connection with proof of alien authorship
16. Use a simple English substitution cipher as the alien language
17. Use faux-Latin merely to communicate institutional age
18. Present every translation with equal certainty
19. Explain every ritual as either definitely magical or definitely meaningless

**#14 deserves emphasis.** No Algonquian, Anishinaabe, Potawatomi, or
Miami-Illinois material — no vocabulary, no phonological texture, no
"inspired-by" borrowing. The Chicago anchor shapes HEL's institutional history.
It does not supply alien source material. Treat this as absolute.

## 4. The ambition, reconciled with the canon

The project goal is a language that rivals Tolkien's. The canon requires a corpus
that is **fragmentary and resistant to complete translation**. These appear to
conflict. They do not, and the resolution is the central design idea of this
project:

> **We build a complete, rigorous, internally consistent system. We then present
> it through the fog of HEL's incomplete and contested reconstruction.**

The depth is real and total in the design bible. The *presentation* is partial.
Fragmentariness is a **presentation layer over a complete system, never an excuse
for a shallow one.** A gap in the in-world corpus must correspond to something we
actually designed and deliberately withheld — never to something we failed to work out.

This also buys the diachronic depth that makes Tolkien's languages feel real,
without naming the builders. Instead of a reconstructed proto-language with named
daughters, we have **corpus strata**: material that differs systematically across
nodes and eras, which HEL scholars variously read as chronological change,
functional register, dialect, or unrelated systems wrongly grouped together.
That is the actual epistemic situation of Linear B, Hittite, and Etruscan
scholarship — and it is *more* interesting than Tolkien's model, because the
reconstruction is contested in-world.

**Corollary:** HEL's linguists are characters. Competing analyses, deprecated
readings, confidence levels, and professional grudges are part of the deliverable.

## 5. Canon-derived design pressures (handoff §6)

The language's distinctive features must arise from these, not from decoration:

- **Directed motion.** One-way edges. Distinguish permitted traversal, hypothetical
  reverse motion, arrival, departure, intermediate traversal, and recurrence at a
  similar-but-not-identical coordinate. A generic "go" is insufficient.
- **Spacetime as one coordinate.** Not "where" + "when" but a composite
  event-location. Distinguish same-place/different-time, same-time/different-place,
  same-node/different-traversal, and predicted vs. observed vs. intended destination.
- **Loop topology.** Sequence, adjacency, junction, convergence, recurrence,
  inaccessible reverse edges, nested cycles, path dependence, route completion.
- **Evidentiality and uncertainty.** Central, not decorative. Observed vs.
  instrument-confirmed vs. repeated-but-unexplained vs. inferred vs. archive-recovered
  vs. provisionally translated vs. ritually preserved without known meaning.
- **Causality under constraint.** Cause inferred across a loop; consequence
  preceding departure; precaution against loop effects; correlation HEL refuses
  to call causal.
- **Stewardship not mastery.** Possession vs. temporary custody vs. authorized
  access vs. responsibility for care vs. containment without ownership vs.
  observation without interference.
- **Secrecy.** Clearance-limited terms, cover terms, archival names that outlived
  their explanations, code words with deliberately mundane literal meanings.
- **Ritual without confirmed mysticism.** Formulae phonologically conserved,
  semantically opaque, retained because deviation correlates with failure.

## 6. Aesthetic direction (handoff §7.1, Authority C)

Ancient without faux-Latin. Alien without unpronounceable noise. Precise about
relation, path, observation, and state. Poor at mapping onto human tense and
location. Capable of terse operational forms.

**Explicitly forbidden:** apostrophe-sprinkling, gratuitous harsh clusters, and a
list of fantasy honorifics. *The alien quality must arise from conceptual
structure, not orthographic costume.* If it reads as alien only because of how
it is spelled, it has failed.

## 7. Four layers — keep them visibly distinct at all times

1. **Underlying system** — the actual nonhuman structure, only partly reconstructable
2. **HEL scholarly transliteration** — standardized representation of recurring units
3. **HEL field pronunciation** — a speakable approximation, possibly phonetically wrong
4. **English gloss** — operational translation ("outbound node," "do not traverse")

Readable dialogue must never imply perfect decipherment. A field agent says a
transliterated term; a linguist notes in a footnote that it may encode topology
rather than meaning "route" at all.

## 8. Every corpus example carries

native/reconstructed script · transliteration · morpheme-by-morpheme gloss
(Leipzig rules) · natural English translation · **confidence rating** ·
**canon note identifying new assumptions and their authority level**

## 9. Process (handoff §10) — we are in Phase 1

**Phase 1 — Canon alignment.** State the target. List every new assumption.
Separate cosmology-exposing from production-only assumptions. Present 2–3
architectures. Recommend one. **Wait for approval.**

Phase 2 — Specification. Phase 3 — Applied corpus. Phase 4 — Production package.

**Do not generate a dictionary. Do not begin Phase 2 before approval.**
