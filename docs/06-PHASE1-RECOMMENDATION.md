# Phase 1 Recommendation — Language Target, Assumptions, Architecture

**For approval.** Nothing here is canon. Authority levels per handoff §2 throughout;
unlabeled claims are **C** (this document's synthesis). No A or B material is created
here. `D-resolved` is not an authority level and does not appear in this document —
two of the three architectures invented it, and it is a self-issued approval gate.

**Governing:** `docs/00-CANON-HANDOFF.md`. **Standard for later rounds:** §7 below.

---

## The decision, in sixty seconds

**Target:** recovered Black Box substrate + HEL transliteration, annotation and
operational register (handoff §9.1 default). **Architecture: A (Path-Primary), amended
— "A′."** A wins on handoff §9.5: it is the only proposal where the alien text *is* the
map, so reading correctly changes what you do. It also derives an Authority A canon
fact as a theorem of its coordinate system, and it is the cheapest to produce by a
wide margin. **From B we import four things** — discharge on graded edges, the
unentitled-grade marker, recurrence scope on negation, and a stated attrition rule —
each landing in a slot A already has, and **we explicitly refuse B's nesting syntax,
its chancery register, and its phonemic vowel length** (§4.4). **C is out** on canon
score, cost, and its own admission that it is weakest if players must solve it; we
salvage its documentation standard. **Two hard preconditions before Phase 2:** A has
never been canon-audited and must be (§5 item 0), and the transliteration layer must
pass a collision screen — `kum` and `we` in particular are unshippable (§4.5). **Six
questions need your answer** (§6 Tier 1); everything else has a recommended default.

## 1. The language target

**Design the recovered Black Box substrate, delivered as a coordinated package with
HEL's transliteration, annotation, and operational register.** This is handoff §9.1's
recommended default, and it survives contact with three worked architectures.

The other three are rejected for stated reasons, not by omission:

| Target | Why not |
| --- | --- |
| **Argot alone** | An English-derived register cannot carry obligatory categories English lacks, so it cannot produce engineered untranslatability (benchmark §4.10) — the only mechanism that makes translation *demonstrably* lossy rather than merely awkward. Necessary component; insufficient target. |
| **Entity language** | Prohibited shortcut #13. Entity nature and communicative capacity are Authority D. Designing it now settles what the entities are by the back door. |
| **Substrate without the HEL layers** | Handoff §5.2's four layers are what stop readable dialogue from implying decipherment. Without layer 3 explicitly marked as HEL's convention, field pronunciation becomes evidence about the source — the trap at benchmark §4.13, which silently resolves handoff §5.3. |

The coordinated package is also the only target serving all of handoff §9.5's surfaces
from one build: the substrate supplies inscriptions, the register supplies everything a
human says aloud.

**One consequence, stated now because it recurs.** In all three architectures, roots
designate conditions or crossings, not names. **The corpus contains no proper name for
any node, artifact, or entity and cannot be made to produce one.** Correct for handoff
§7.5; the largest product gap in the set. It is solved in HEL's English and catalog
layers, which §5 promotes to a first-class Phase 2 deliverable.

## 3. The three architectures

### A — Path-Primary Notation

The clause is a directed edge between two node-states, spelled across the gap between
them and closed by a warrant stating how the record knows. An edge is one morpheme in
two halves — a tail suffix on the origin, a head prefix on the destination — so
orientation is carried by *which half lands where*, and no well-formed segment can be
made without committing to a direction. A broken half is diagnostic: a fragment ending
in a tail tells HEL a node is missing and constrains what class it was, which is how
the corpus reconstructs graph structure it does not contain. The coordinate is a single
three-slot enclitic (LOCUS · PASS · PHASE) in which neither English *where* nor *when*
is a primitive, and every unfilled slot must be **overtly marked unfilled** — you
cannot underspecify quietly. Canon A then falls out as a theorem: "return to 2024"
specifies PASS and PHASE, LOCUS is not derivable from them, so the traveler emerges
somewhere unspecified. Negation is edge-scoped and four-way; command grades sit on the
edge because there is no verb.

**Canon audit verdict: NOT AUDITED.** The one material gap in the evidence before me,
and I will not paper over it. Read against the standards applied to B and C, A carries
at least four of the same defects: the invented `D-resolved` level; the
operational-correlation confirmation channel asserted but unledgered; paradigm tables
with uncontested single glosses and no grade column (shortcut #18 defended at the
example level, violated at the paradigm level); and the phonological collisions below.
**Audit is a precondition of Phase 2, not a Phase 2 task.**

**Production ranking: 1st (design, 8/10) · 2nd (voice, 6.5/10).**

**Honest weaknesses.** It states in writing that it cannot produce a lament — "if a
writer sits down to write Namárië in this system, there is nothing to write it with" —
and on the evidence that is true rather than modest. Every trace is a line with notches,
so assets are silhouette-identical at prop and UI scale. `kum` is an English obscenity
in its first spoken example and stacks with `hos`; `we`, the obligatory gap marker and
by design the most frequent morpheme in the language, is the English first-person
plural, producing `we-nu-we`. And it presses hard against shortcut #2: a grammar that
encodes topology this cleanly makes the Box look solvable. That is the failure mode it
will actually die of.

### B — Liturgical-Performative Substrate

There is no bare proposition. Every well-formed unit is an act — opened, held by a
stated standing, scoped over an extent, closed by a discharge condition — and
descriptions exist only sealed inside an attesting frame. The corpus does not tell HEL
what the network is; it licenses, forbids, binds, consigns and releases passage, and
HEL reconstructs the map as the shadow of a permission structure. Frames nest, and
nesting is scope. The four negations sit at four different structural levels rather
than in one paradigm — the cleanest untranslatability mechanism in the set. Discharge
replaces tense: an act is spent, standing, conditioned, lapsed or unstated, and
nineteen frames stand with no attested remission over extents HEL cannot identify. Its
taphonomic argument is the best available: HEL holds thresholds, and thresholds carry
the texts that govern passage.

**Canon audit verdict: REPAIRABLE, canon score 4/10.** Three BLOCKING: silent resolution
of the confirmation question with the denominator concealed; layer-3 transliteration
promoted into a reconstruction of the source's phonology (vowel agreement and
contrastive length as source-level exponents — a vocal tract by implication); and the
oral-formulae channel assigned a stratum, which determines its origin, then mislabelled
production-only. Plus eleven SERIOUS, including an undetected bind/consign collision
across 39% of the corpus and a Polynesian phonotype under a written certificate saying
no real language supplied material.

**Production ranking: 2nd (design, 6/10) · 1st (voice, 7/10).**

**Honest weaknesses.** Its own §13.1 concedes the decisive point: the alien text is not
the map, so a correct reading yields a yes/no gate — a sign, not a puzzle. Nesting makes
layout content-dependent: no font, no fixed-size asset, illegible past depth 2 at UI
scale. Discharge rides on phonemic vowel length, which anglophone actors cannot
reliably reproduce across pickups, and it carries the difference between *spent* and
*in force until remitted*. Four lavatory-or-nursery collisions sit in its four
most-repeated slots, one of them inside its emotional centrepiece.

### C — Stratified Corpus / Two-System Hypothesis

Two structurally different systems that HEL's catalog has filed as one for sixty years,
showing seven regular formal correspondences; whether those mean descent, elaboration,
contact, or nothing is the field's central dispute and stays unresolved. The coordinate
is inflected and the predicate is bare. It reproduces Tolkien's actual engine —
relatedness you can *work out*, applied by the reader to a form they have never seen —
while removing the part canon forbids, a named ancestral people. Three named
reconstructions, each with conceded weaknesses, one of which is right about methodology
and will lose because winning costs more than the institution can pay.

**Canon audit verdict: REPAIRABLE, canon score 3/10 — lowest.** Two BLOCKING: its
headline safety guarantee ("producing builder-lore requires breaking the grammar") is
false and contradicted by at least eight of its own morpheme definitions, which is the
single most dangerous item in the set because it invites Phase 2 authors to stop
policing themselves; and prohibited shortcut #14 via a Polynesian inventory under a
false compliance certificate. Independently, the voice review found two sample forms
that are real Indigenous-language material — one the autonym of a living North American
Pueblo people, one documented Quechua — inside a document formally certifying that none
was used.

**Production ranking: 3rd (design, 4/10) · 3rd (voice, 5/10).**

**Honest weaknesses.** Its own §11.5: "if players must be able to solve it, C is the
weakest of the three," because puzzles need unique answers and its proposition is that
answers are contested. 2.2–2.5× cost with the multiplier in the correspondence layer,
where there is no cheap edit after Phase 2. Three asset families. Requires a permanent
curator. Cosmological exposure is concentrated and untrimmable: refuse either of two
channel assumptions and C does not survive in this form.

## 4. The recommendation

**Adopt Architecture A as the structural base, amended by four named imports from B and
one hard phonological gate. Call it A′.** This is a hybrid, and it is specified below
rather than gestured at.

### 4.1 Why A wins

**1. Handoff §9.5 decides it.** "Should correct interpretation affect route navigation
or remain atmospheric?" A is the only architecture where the alien text *is* the map,
so a correct reading changes what a character or player does. B says it itself: "B's
puzzle is *may I*; A's is *how do I get there*. Those are different games." The smaller
game is a locked door with lore on it, and indifference kills products as reliably as
frustration.

**2. A's coordinate derives canon rather than accommodating it.** "Return to 2024 may
emerge in Los Angeles" is a theorem of the three-slot coordinate. No other proposal
derives an Authority A statement from its grammar.

**3. Obligatory overt ignorance-marking is the deepest single idea in the set.** A
machine leaves a field null; a careless speaker leaves it out; here, *not knowing is
something you must say*. It yields the redaction convention for free, answers shortcut
#18 at the grammatical rather than the editorial level, and is coherent under all three
of A's interpretive schools.

**4. Production, and it is not close.** One asset family serves inscription, map, UI
chrome, prop etching and signage, because a route trace and a route diagram are the same
object. Transliteration is ASCII-clean — no diacritics, no apostrophes — so it is
typeable, searchable, font-safe and transcribable in a forum thread, which is how the
Fez and Tunic communities actually worked. Authoring is slot-filling a generalist can do
in minutes, and it is **machine-lintable**: writer output validates in CI instead of
waiting on a specialist.

**5. Its phonological rationale is the strongest #14 inoculation in the set.** A 1908
code for radio and telephone work, voiced stops and voiceless fricatives excluded
because they are the first casualties of a noisy channel, minuted and still in force —
every sound has a stated engineering reason, so nobody is tempted to reach for a real
language for texture. It also permits codas, which breaks the Polynesian silhouette
that made B and C non-compliant on *result* even where they were compliant on process.

### 4.2 Why B loses

On the navigation question above, and on three production facts it concedes: nesting is
content-dependent layout (no font, no fixed asset, illegible past depth 2); its core
categories are *semantic* claims, so a reviewer who declines the performative reading
has rejected the architecture rather than a detail; and its English register is
chancery-plus-vestry, which its own §13.3 calls a defence rather than an absolution and
which drifts to fantasy-holy-order within a quarter.

**B is not discarded.** It holds the one thing A demonstrably lacks — emotional
machinery — and §4.4 takes it.

### 4.3 Why C loses

On canon (lowest score, and its BLOCKING finding is a *false certificate of structural
immunity* — the most dangerous possible artifact in a project already scrapped once for
builder invention), on production (last on both rankings, 2.2–2.5× cost, three asset
families, permanent curator), and on handoff §9.5 (its own §11.5 concedes it is weakest
if players must solve it).

**Salvage two things from C regardless.** First, its **documentation standard**: it
alone prints IPA for a full utterance, specifies vowel qualities, forbids reduction
("no reduction, ever" — the most useful single direction in the three files), and gives
one line in two house accents whose difference is audible, coachable and castable.
Adopt it verbatim for A′, justified by A's committee-minutes rationale. Second, note
what we are giving up: C's comparative engine — a rule the reader applies to an unseen
form and gets right — is the highest-quality single mechanic in the set and is genuinely
unavailable to a one-system design. **We decline it on cost and solvability,
knowingly.** The cheap partial substitute is already in P3: give one stratum a
stateable conditioned difference from another, so a reader who has seen two examples can
predict the third.

### 4.4 The hybrid, specified

Four imports. Each lands in a slot A already has; **none imports B's frame syntax,
which is the part that breaks layout, fonts and actors.** Each must pass A's own
Three-School Test (readable under Utterance, Instrument and Palimpsest) — stated per
import, because that test is A's compliance mechanism and an unchecked import would
void it.

| # | Import | Where it lands in A | Three-School check | What it buys |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Discharge is what that restriction presupposes. | Utterance: an act's force. Instrument: a lifetime flag on a permission record. syntax (content-dependent layout, no font, no fixed-size asset); B's act-class English
gloss register (register drift, its own §13.3); **B's phonemic vowel length as a
semantic carrier** (English has no phonemic length; anglophone actors will not hold the
contrast across pickups, and it would be carrying *spent* versus *in force until
remitted*); B's strictly open-syllable inventory (Polynesian silhouette — A's codas are
the fix and must be retained for that reason as well as their stated one).

### 4.5 The hard gate before any Phase 2 form is written

All three phonetic layers were written with no read-aloud pass and no collision screen,
and the failures cluster in the highest-frequency morphemes. In A′ this is cheap to fix
and expensive to ship: layer 2 is Authority C and in-world a human committee's product,
so respelling disturbs no structural claim.

**Known-defective units in A, to be respelled:** `kum` (English obscenity, in the first
spoken example, and it stacks with `hos`); **`we`** (English pronoun — and it is the
obligatory gap marker, by design the most frequent morpheme in the language, producing
`we-nu-we`); `-mir` (the Mir space station, in a spacefaring property); `-nas` (a very
high-frequency Arabic noun, in the custodial formula); `ramaki` (reads as a Hindi
devotional form, also in the custodial formula); `-per`/`-num` together (*per annum*,
against A's own claim to exclude faux-Latin); `otera` and `hemasu` (both read as
Japanese, undercutting "belongs to nobody"); `sirwe`, `kera`, `pos`, `pon`.

**Standing rule (C).** No unit enters the transliteration standard until screened
against English, Spanish, Mandarin, Hindi, Arabic and French for obscene, comic, brand
and real-person-name collisions — **and separately and non-negotiably against real
Indigenous language material worldwide.** The screen is a Phase 2 deliverable with a
date, the families screened, and a rejects file in the canon ledger.

**The certification language changes too.** Never "no real-world language supplied
material": that is a claim about process, which one accidental hit converts into an
apparent falsehood — worse than the hit alone, and exactly how C's compliance statement
became an aggravating factor. Write instead: *"No real-world language was consulted.
Every form was screened against [families] on [date]; screen and rejects are in the
ledger."*

### 4.6 What A′ still cannot do

**It still cannot produce a lament, and the imports do not fix that.** H1 and H2 supply
dread and transgression; neither supplies interiority, address, memory, comparison or
judgment of value. The honest position is A's own: emotional payload comes from
provenance and frame, not semantics — a formula recited by crews who cannot translate
it, over the dead; a warrant nobody can pronounce, said by everyone who goes out; a
discipline whose stratigraphy its own physics invalidates. Benchmark C12 licenses this
route explicitly, and the fact that every genuinely hair-raising line in all three
proposals is in HEL's English is evidence the route works.

**It must not be assumed to work.** Phase 2 carries a standing task: write one line in
A′ that survives being read aloud by an actor, and judge it out loud rather than on the
page. If that fails, A's designed reserve — structurally identified withheld space — is
where a non-topological register would have to go, and that is an architecture
decision, not a content decision.

## 5. What approval unblocks — the Phase 2 work plan

Ordered. Items 0 and 1 are preconditions and can start the day approval lands.

**0. Canon audit of Architecture A, to the standard applied to B and C.** First and
non-negotiable. Known targets: delete the `D-resolved` coinage and re-label C; ledger
the operational-correlation channel as X4, publish its denominator, print the
selection-bias objection; add a grade column to every paradigm table, contest at least
two cells, and leave at least one closed-class cell unglossed (every function word in A
is currently solved, which is the tidiest thing in the document); re-classify the
human-transmission channel's origin as cosmology-exposing and add the fourth skeptical
position; ledger the script; give the taphonomic argument a form that runs under all
three of A's schools, not just one.

**1. The naming layer, as a first-class deliverable.** Architecture-independent —
starts immediately. The corpus cannot produce a proper name, four English field names
exist across ~190KB of proposals, and "CSC-0012" is not a t-shirt. Deliver **generative
rules, not a list** (benchmark C13 scores a fixed list 3/5): how HEL coins a field name
from a trace shape, a find circumstance, a failure, or a deprecated reading; how catalog
IDs form and why they survive a withdrawn gloss. A's "the Ladder — named for a
now-deprecated reading, unrenameable because the routes are catalogued under it" is the
best naming *rule* in the three documents. Turn it into a generator.

**2. Transliteration standard**, after the §4.5 screen, with documented lossiness and
the units carrying no assigned value.

**3. Field pronunciation to C's documentation standard**: IPA for full utterances,
specified vowel qualities, no-reduction rule, two branch traditions as an audible and
coachable contrast, 1+ contested unit.

**4. The complete internal grammar as a sealed file** — morphology, syntax, coordinate,
traversal, four negations, evidentiality and confidence, register differences (archive /
laboratory / field), with H1–H3 fully specified including attestation counts, stratum
distributions and provenance lines. Predicates defined by argument frame, not by gloss.

**5. The attrition model and render-then-damage pipeline** (H4) with published counts,
so C4's audit is mechanical rather than a matter of authorial care.

**6. The reading history**: named analysts with dates, affiliation, method and bias;
1+ superseded reading with its specific killing evidence; 1+ live dispute the project
holds no private answer to.

**7. Script and field hand**, one asset family. **Condition of approval (C,
production-only):** each edge class gets a distinct cluster or terminal silhouette so
class reads before any detail resolves, and the coordinate cartouche gets a fixed outer
form — otherwise every asset is a line with notches at prop distance and at 24px, which
A's §11.6 concedes and answers only with a style guide.

**8. The sealed-withholding register**: every published gap mapped to an internal entry
stating what exists, why it is withheld, and the in-world cause. Auditable by count.

**Not in Phase 2:** the lexicon. Phase 3 is the controlled corpus of handoff §10's nine
item types; Phase 4 is the production package.

## 7. Scoring rubric — the standard for later rounds

From `docs/04-benchmark.md` §5. Fourteen weighted criteria summing to 100, scored 1–5,
with 2 and 4 interpolated between the stated descriptors.

**Gate 0 — the nineteen prohibited shortcuts, as a pre-scoring pass/fail.** A violation
is blocking. Do not score a deliverable that fails Gate 0.

| | Criterion — what a 5 requires | Wt |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **C1** | **Canon compliance / authority labeling.** Every non-A claim labeled at first assertion; C and D items name a rejected alternative; zero plausibility-as-warrant reasoning; assumption list cross-referenced to the ledger. **Score 1 = Gate 1 failure**, including any D item silently resolved. | 12 |
| **C2** | **Builder opacity.** Passes the Builder-Inference Test with zero leaks; every feature that could imply a builder trait carries 2+ competing in-world explanations, 1+ non-biological; layer 3 explicitly HEL's convention, never evidence about the source. **Score 1 = Gate 1 failure.** | 10 |
| **C3** | **Productive ambiguity.** 2+ fully specified readings per ambiguous item, each coherent with the whole grammar, each with a *different* operational consequence; discriminating evidence named and its absence explained; 1+ reading falsified in-corpus and retained as deprecated; 1+ live dispute the project holds no private answer to. | 9 |
| **C4** | **Hidden-system integrity.** Complete internal system as a file; every fragment derived from it with a rule trace on demand; documented attrition model; audit showing zero fragments violating hidden rules; every gap mapped to a sealed entry. **≤2 triggers Gate 2 (cap 74).** | 9 |
| **C5** | **Engineered untranslatability.** 3+ obligatory substrate categories English lacks; every gloss states its residue; 1+ text untranslatable without a choice that changes operational meaning, *shown not claimed*; a no-gloss zone under catalog IDs. **≤2 triggers Gate 2.** | 8 |
| **C6** | **Morphosyntactic rigor.** Rules productive under test (reviewer supplies a gloss, gets a derived form with trace); round-trip test with a second analyst; 3+ multi-clause texts with defined subordination; negation distinguishes impossible/forbidden/unavailable/unverified; predicates defined by argument frame, not English gloss. | 8 |
| **C7** | **Corpus realism.** Taphonomy with consequences visible in length, register, condition and strata; provenance line on every item; register skewed administrative and formulaic; 1+ formula recognizable as a frame without translation; stated size consistent with what is shown. | 7 |
| **C8** | **Reading-history depth.** 3+ named analysts with dated readings, affiliation, method, identifiable bias; 1+ reading superseded with the *specific* killing evidence; 1+ disagreement unresolved anywhere; deprecated readings retained. | 7 |
| **C9** | **Phonological / transliteration integrity.** Inventory and phonotactics with distributional rationale, every form conforming; transliteration an explicit mapping with documented lossiness; field pronunciation explicitly HEL's, with 1+ variant tradition and 1+ contested unit; no apostrophe-decoration, no English cipher (#16), no faux-Latin (#17). | 6 |
| **C10** | **Layer discipline.** Every citation labeled by layer; no unmarked layer-mixing; field dialogue never implies decipherment; 1+ documented case where field form and scholarly analysis conflict, left unresolved. | 6 |
| **C11** | **Solvability footholds.** An outsider using only published material derives 1+ nontrivial correct reading, and that path has been tested on someone; 1+ fragment confirmable against an independently known referent; repetition density sufficient for distributional inference. | 6 |
| **C12** | **Emotional charge via provenance.** 3+ items whose weight comes from circumstances of recovery or use rather than semantics; 1+ item whose meaning is unknown and whose loss is felt; no item dependent on a grandiose English gloss. | 5 |
| **C13** | **Production fitness.** Glyphs legible at UI sizes with a construction grid; field hand alongside the high-concept form; performer pronunciation guide; font/asset path; **generative** naming rules, not a list; canon ledger with date and status per term. | 4 |
| **C14** | **Aesthetic distinctiveness.** Identifiable from three forms; character describable non-circularly (not "alien," not "ancient"); not confusable with Elvish/Klingon/Valyrian/Na'vi/generic sci-fi glyphwork; distinguishing property **structural, not orthographic**. | 3 |

**Gates.** Gate 1: a score of 1 on C1 or C2 fails the deliverable outright. Gate 2: a
score of ≤2 on C4 or C5 caps the total at 74.
**Thresholds.** ≥85 accept · 70–84 revise · <70 reject.
**Procedure.** Two independent evaluators. Deltas ≥2 reconciled by citing line numbers;
on failure to reconcile, the lower score stands and the disagreement is recorded in
`/critique/`. Every score of 1 or 2 must cite a specific instance. Phase applicability
per the benchmark's P1–P4 matrix, with proportional weight redistribution for criteria
not scored in a given phase.

**Standing countermeasures carried into every round** (benchmark §6): the inference
ledger; an adversarial builder-profile pass; the two-explanation requirement with at
least one non-biological; the **rationale ban** (the design bible may not contain a
functional justification for a substrate feature — justifications live in the mouths of
named HEL analysts, where a second analyst can disagree); the sealed-withholding
register auditable by count; on-demand derivation with rule trace during review; the
round-trip test on 5+ items; the no-orphan-morpheme audit; the foothold quota; ambiguity
triage; the outsider decipherment trial as a stop-ship condition; and a falsification
budget of at least one retired in-world reading per round.

## 8. What approval means

**Approves:** the target (§1); the assumption ledger (§2) with its cosmology split;
Architecture A′ per §4, including the four named imports and the four named exclusions;
the Phase 2 plan (§5); the Tier 1 answers and any Tier 2 exceptions (§6); the rubric
(§7) as the standard for every later round.

**Does not approve:** any lexicon, any glyph, any Authority D resolution, or the
Authority-B-dependent custodial material, which stands or falls with handoff §4.3–4.4.

**Phase 2 does not begin until Architecture A is audited to the standard applied to B
and C.** B scored 4 and C scored 3 against this canon, both after presenting clean
self-checks. A's self-check is written in the same voice.
